# INSYT.BAU Academic OS — Hackathon Presentation & Demo Script

## Duration: 3-5 Minutes Pitch & Live Demonstration

---

### Act 1: The Problem (0:00 – 0:45)
**Speaker:**
> *"Bangladesh Agricultural University in Mymensingh is the country's flagship agricultural research institution, with 6 faculties and 44 departments. Yet, student life is severely fragmented:*
> 1. *Routines and exam circulars are stuck in complex PDF notices and physical board printouts with scheduling clashes.*
> 2. *High-stakes oral viva exams cause intense performance anxiety with zero simulated practice.*
> 3. *Field pathology specimens are difficult to identify and map to practical lab exercises.*
> 4. *Students struggle to calculate standing under the unique BAU 10/20/70 Academic Ordinance.*
>
> *Today, we present **INSYT.BAU** — the world's first AI-Native Academic Operating System engineered specifically for Bangladesh Agricultural University, powered by **Google Gemini** and **ElevenLabs**."*

---

### Act 2: Gemini Multimodal Routine Intelligence (0:45 – 1:30)
**Action on Screen:** Navigate to `/academy/schedule`. Click **"LOAD SAMPLE BAU L2S1 PDF"** (or drop a departmental routine notice).
**Speaker:**
> *"Here in Schedule Intelligence, a student doesn't need to manually type in their timetable. They simply upload their department's official PDF circular.*
> *Google Gemini 1.5 Flash immediately parses the complex timetable matrix, extracts course codes (like AAS 2107 and AE 2111), allocates Group A vs Group B practical sessions, and flags room and time conflicts automatically."*

---

### Act 3: ElevenLabs Spoken AI Viva Voce Room (1:30 – 2:30)
**Action on Screen:** Navigate to `/academy/viva`. Select course `AAS 2107` and click **"ENTER ORAL DEFENSE ROOM"**.
**Speaker:**
> *"Now for our crown jewel: the **Conversational AI Viva Room**. Viva voce counts for major marks at BAU, but students have no way to practice speaking under pressure.*
> *Watch this: ElevenLabs speaks the oral question out loud with a natural academic faculty voice. The student presses the microphone button and responds verbally.*
> *Immediately, Gemini 1.5 evaluates four core dimensions: Technical Accuracy, Conceptual Depth, Logical Reasoning, and Spoken Fluency. At the end of the session, the student receives an official Viva Scorecard and a direct 12-minute remedial sprint link for their weakest topic."*

---

### Act 4: Gemini Multimodal Field & Practical Specimen Lab (2:30 – 3:15)
**Action on Screen:** Navigate to `/academy/field-lab`. Select **Rice Blast** or **Liver Fluke specimen**.
**Speaker:**
> *"In our **Field & Specimen Intelligence Lab**, students upload photos from the BAU Central Farm or laboratory microscope slides.*
> *Gemini Vision diagnoses the pathogen or parasite, cites its binomial name, highlights visible lesion patterns, maps it to the exact BAU course module (e.g. Plant Pathology), and gives step-by-step practical laboratory protocols."*

---

### Act 5: 10/20/70 Ordinance Lab & Academic DAG (3:15 – 4:00)
**Action on Screen:** Navigate to `/academy/assessment`, adjust 10/20/70 sliders, then jump to `/academy/skills` and `/opportunities`.
**Speaker:**
> *"Under the official BAU Ordinance (10% Attendance + 20% Continuous Assessment + 70% Final Exam), students can interactively simulate their required final exam score to achieve their Dean's Award or 3.75+ CGPA.*
> *Furthermore, our **Academic Dependency Graph** connects prerequisite courses directly to career unlocks in the BCS Agriculture Cadre and NARS research institutes like BARI and BRRI.*
> *INSYT.BAU is not a generic tutorial site — it is an end-to-end AI operating system built for the next generation of agricultural scientists."*
